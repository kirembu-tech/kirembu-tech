Geological Mapping & GIS Analysis

Overview

This project demonstrates the application of Geographic Information Systems (GIS) and geological interpretation techniques in geological mapping and spatial analysis.

The project involved the interpretation and analysis of geological and topographic information to produce maps and identify important geological and structural features.

Objectives

- To analyse geological and spatial data using GIS tools.
- To identify and interpret geological structures and lithological units.
- To process Digital Elevation Model (DEM) data.
- To produce geological and thematic maps.
- To apply spatial analysis techniques to geological problems.

Tools & Software

- QGIS
- ArcGIS / ArcMap
- Google Earth Pro
- Microsoft Excel

Methodology

The workflow involved acquisition and preparation of spatial data, processing of Digital Elevation Model (DEM) data, geological interpretation, digitization of geological features, spatial analysis, and cartographic presentation.

DEM-derived products such as hillshade and slope maps were used to enhance terrain visualization and support the interpretation of geological and structural features.

Key Outputs

- Geological maps
- Topographic and thematic maps
- DEM-derived hillshade maps
- Slope maps
- Digitized geological structures
- Spatial analysis outputs

Skills Demonstrated

- Geological interpretation
- GIS data processing
- Spatial analysis
- DEM processing
- Geological digitization
- Cartographic visualization
- Geospatial data management

Conclusion

The project demonstrates how geological knowledge can be integrated with GIS and spatial analysis techniques to investigate geological features and communicate geological information through professional maps and visualizations.
